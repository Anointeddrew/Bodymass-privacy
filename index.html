Privacy Policy

App: Body Mass Calculator
Effective date: 14 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Body Mass Calculator app handles information when you use it. The app does not require you to create an account or sign in.

1. Information You Enter

The app allows you to enter measurements such as height and weight so it can calculate your Body Mass Index (BMI). These measurements are used to provide the calculation and related results within the app.

Based on the app's described local-only operation, the measurements you enter are processed on your device and are not sent to the app developer or stored on a developer-controlled server.

2. Personal Information and Accounts

The app does not ask you to register, create an account, or provide your name, email address, phone number, or other account details to use its BMI calculator.

3. Data Sharing

The app developer does not sell, rent, or share the measurements you enter with third parties. The app does not transmit those measurements to the developer.

4. Analytics, Advertising, and Third-Party Services

This policy assumes that the app does not include third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or other SDKs that collect information from your device. If any such service is added, this Privacy Policy must be updated to identify the service, explain what information it collects, and link to its privacy policy.

5. Data Retention and Security

The app developer does not maintain a server-side database of the measurements entered into the calculator. Information handled temporarily by the app is subject to the security of your device and its operating system. You are responsible for keeping your device secure.

6. Children's Privacy

The app is not designed to collect personal information from children. Because the app does not require account registration and, as described above, does not transmit calculator measurements to the developer, it does not knowingly collect children's personal information.

7. Health Information Disclaimer

BMI is a screening measurement calculated from height and weight. It is not a medical diagnosis and does not, by itself, determine whether a person is healthy. The app is provided for general informational purposes only and is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for advice about your health.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's features or information practices change. Any revised version will be published at this page with an updated effective date. Please review this page periodically.
